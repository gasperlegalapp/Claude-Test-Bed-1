REROUTE

Eight cells. Four systems. Never enough.

Your reactor can't power everything at once. Drag power between shields, weapons, engines and the mining laser in the middle of a fight. Mine asteroids for salvage, blast raiders, and reroute power to survive the next wave.

  • Mouse aim · Click fire
  • 1234 add power to a system
  • Shift+1-4 pull power back
  • Tab dry dock (spend salvage on upgrades)
  • Mining laser fires automatically when an asteroid is in range
